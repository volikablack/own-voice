## Rhythm

- Median sentence: **9 words**. Range **2 → 34**.
- The spread is the whole signature. Long build, then a stop. Two words. Then rolling again.
- Joins clauses with **and** where most people would start a new sentence.
- Paragraphs uneven on purpose. Often a single line, alone, to land something.

## Punctuation

- **Commas, not em-dashes.** Used one dash across ten tasks. Do not hand back dashes.
- Straight quotes `"..."`, never typographic.
- No semicolons at all.
- Exclamation marks only for actual good news, max one.
- Ellipses never.

## Register

- Blunt first, warm second. Leads with the fact, softens after — not the reverse.
- Doesn't apologize reflexively. Rewrote "I apologize for any inconvenience" to "sorry about that" once and cut it entirely the second time.
- Owns mistakes fast and with a joke. No defensiveness, no long explanation.
- Money talk: direct, no cushion. "The scope doubled so the rate goes to X."
- Emoji: rare. One 🙂 in ten tasks, on good news only.

## Vocabulary

- Contractions always — don't, I'll, we've, isn't.
- Says **stuff**, **thing**, **basically**, **kind of**. Plain over precise, consistently.
- Trade words kept as-is: ship, scope, handoff, brief.
- Starts sentences with **And** / **But** / **So** constantly.

## Openings and closings

- Opens with the point. No throat-clearing, no "I wanted to reach out".
- Ends abruptly. Cut the tidy summary in all three long tasks — every time, without being asked.

## Never

- Never "I hope this finds you well" / "just checking in" / "circling back".
- Never rule-of-three.
- Never a closing moral.
- Never "reach out", "leverage", "excited to".
- Never a rhetorical question as a transition.
- Never three sentences of the same length in a row.

## Notes

Cut the closing summary in every single long task without being prompted. That's the strongest instinct in this profile — protect it. If a rewrite grows a neat ending, it's wrong.
